Artificial intelligence (AI) is intelligence demonstrated by machines, as opposed to intelligence displayed by humans or other animals. 
The term "artificial intelligence" is often used to describe machines that mimic cognitive functions such as learning and problem solving. 
Recent advancements in AI have led to breakthroughs in natural language processing, computer vision, and autonomous systems.
Some of the most popular applications of AI today include virtual assistants, recommendation engines, and generative AI models.
